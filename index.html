The MCT Philosophy

Three Pillars of Change

Our work is built on timeless Indian values — a framework of humanity, culture, and ethics that guides every initiative we undertake.

favorite_border
मानवता

Manvta

Humanity

Humanity is the greatest, most defining quality of our species—the invisible thread that connects us to our fellow human beings, as well as the animals and birds that share our world. We empower the youth with these exact qualities, teaching them that true strength lies in lifting others up and showing mercy to the voiceless.

temple_hindu
संस्कृती

Sanskruti

Cultural Heritage

Culture plays an incredibly major role in connecting diverse people and transforming the human experience. We promote lasting social harmony through community bonding, fostering a sense of unbreakable togetherness through vibrant festival celebrations.

auto_awesome
संस्कार

Sanskar

Values

Value Education (Sanskar) plays a vital, non-negotiable role in both the outer behavior and the inner psychological development of our youth. These foundational values form the bedrock of human bonding to build a secure, empathetic society.

flag
Our Mission

Miam Charitable Trust is a dedicated, rapidly developing NGO making a measurable, positive impact on human lives, animal welfare, and environmental health every single year. We focus our resources on Adivasi (indigenous) empowerment and rural development.

visibility
Our Vision

We operate on the fundamental truth that humans, animals, and the natural environment are deeply and inextricably interconnected. We help marginalized communities take the reins in actively shaping their own successful futures through vital Government schemes.

MIAM Career Academy

Mission Rozgar — Free competitive exam coaching, physical training, and knowledge centres for underprivileged youth. Our academies empower tribal and rural students to achieve government employment and self-reliance.

  • location_on
    Waghera, Nashik — Physical training, free government job exam coaching, and mentorship for tribal youth.
  • location_on
    Chintalpeth, Gadchiroli — Career coaching centre providing examination preparation for rural students.
  • local_library
    Library Academy — Free community libraries in Lahvit & Donwade villages serving hundreds of students.
  • event
    Sunday is Funday — Weekly recreational learning and joy camps for children in Gadchiroli.
eco Environment

हरियाली का संकल्प — Green Revolution

From the Chaprala Wildlife Sanctuary to urban Mumbai, our plantation drives protect ecosystems and combat climate change through community-led conservation.

  • forest
    400+ Trees Planted — Chaprala Wildlife Sanctuary reforestation and biodiversity conservation drives.
  • yard
    Fruit Tree Campaign — "हर आंगन में एक फलदार पेड़" (A fruit tree in every courtyard) in Vadalapeth village.
  • agriculture
    Plant Nursery — Women-led nurseries in Gadchiroli fostering economic empowerment through sustainable agriculture.
  • location_city
    Urban Plantation — Oshiwara Garden, Mumbai — bringing green spaces to urban environments.

Animal Care

Comprehensive care for stray and destitute animals: sterilization drives, vaccination programs, daily feeding rounds, emergency medical treatment, and snake rescue workshops in Chandrapur-Gadchiroli. Every life matters.

The People Behind

Founder & Trustee

Nitu Joshi

Director — Ebina Group of Companies

emoji_events Best Social Worker Award — Mid-Day, 2023

Nitu Joshi is a seasoned "Business and Social Entrepreneur" with over 25 years of experience. She founded Aayush Telefilms and Vedansh Entertainment in 1998 to provide clean, family-oriented entertainment through TV and film, with content aired on major national channels.

workspace_premium Latest Achievements

As Group Director of Ebina Group, she drives the success of Ebina Entertainment, and her recent documentary, "Dharamraobaba Atram – Dilon Ka Raja," Released on Jio Cinema and won Best Documentary at the Cine Dreams International Film Festival 2024.

In 2021, she established "Miam Charitable Trust" to tackle social, environmental, and socio-cultural issues, embodying her belief in "dosro ki liye bhi jeena sikho" (learning to live for others).
