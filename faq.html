info About Mïam Charitable Trust

What is Mïam Charitable Trust? +

Mïam Charitable Trust is a non-profit organization based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, dedicated to uplifting underserved communities—especially rural and tribal (Adivasi) populations. We work across multiple domains including education, healthcare, environmental sustainability, women empowerment, and animal welfare.

Founded in 2021 by social entrepreneur Nitu Joshi, our core philosophy combines Sanskar + Sanskruti + Manavta (Values + Culture + Humanity).

Where does Mïam operate? +

We primarily operate across rural Maharashtra, with a strong focus on:

  • Gadchiroli — Tribal belt, Chintalpeth Academy
  • Nashik — Waghera Career Hub, tree plantation drives
  • Chandrapur — Community health and women empowerment initiatives

Our headquarters is in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

Is Mïam a registered NGO? +

Yes. Mïam Charitable Trust is a fully registered NGO in India, recognized under:

  • 80G Registration — Tax exemption for donor contributions (URN: AAITM1924DF20231)
  • 12A Registration — Non-profit status recognition (URN: AAITM1924DE20221)
  • NITI Aayog NGO Darpan ID — National integration and transparency (UID: MH/2024/0417254)

This makes us eligible for CSR partnerships, government grants, and individual donations with full tax benefits.

Who founded Mïam? +

Nitu Joshi is the founder and director of Mïam Charitable Trust. She is a seasoned social entrepreneur with over 25 years of experience in entertainment, media, and social development. She was recognized as Best Social Worker by Mid-Day in 2023.

Before founding Mïam, Nitu established Aayush Telefilms and Vedansh Entertainment, producing family-oriented content. She also leads the Ebina Group and produced the award-winning documentary "Dharamraobaba Atram – Dilon Ka Raja" (Best Documentary, Cine Dreams International Film Festival 2024), which highlights tribal stories.

school Our Programs & Work

What is the MIAM Academy? +

MIAM Academy is our flagship Mission Rozgar (Mission Employment) initiative. It's a network of free competitive exam coaching centres, physical training facilities, and community libraries in rural and tribal belts.

Key components:

  • Waghera, Nashik Hub — Physical training and police/defense job preparation
  • Chintalpeth, Gadchiroli Centre — Career coaching in one of Maharashtra's most remote districts
  • Library Academy Initiative — Free, fully-stocked community libraries in Lahvit and Donwade villages

These academies help marginalized youth secure stable government employment and pull their families out of generational poverty.

What environmental work does Mïam do? +

Our environmental initiatives focus on sustainable rural ecosystems:

  • Large-scale tree plantation drives — Thousands of trees planted across Nashik and Gadchiroli
  • "A Fruit Tree in Every Courtyard" campaign — Distributing fruit trees to rural households for food security and income generation
  • Wildlife awareness programs — Snake rescue workshops and ecosystem education for tribal youth

We believe sustainable environmental practices are essential for rural development and long-term community prosperity.

Does Mïam work with animals? +

Yes. Our Jiva Daya (Compassion for Life) program focuses on animal welfare:

  • Care for stray and injured animals
  • Sterilization and vaccination drives
  • Rescue and rehabilitation of wildlife
  • Community awareness about animal rights

Humanity extends to all living beings—this is core to our philosophy.

What community programs does Mïam run? +

We run multiple community empowerment programs:

  • Women Empowerment — Public speaking, leadership training, skill development, community awareness on social issues
  • Child Development — Educational activities, Sunday learning programs ("Sunday is Funday"), recreational engagement, nutrition support
  • Health & Social Welfare — Medical camps, food distribution, support to orphanages and old age homes
  • Education Initiatives — Notebook and school kit distribution, school bag donations, child sponsorship programs, school exposure trips

Yes. All donations to Mïam Charitable Trust are eligible for tax exemption under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act.

80G Registration Number: AAITM1924DF20231

You can claim 100% of your donation as a tax deduction. We will provide you with proper documentation for your tax filing.

volunteer_activism Volunteering & Engagement

How can I volunteer with Mïam? +

We'd love your support! You can volunteer by:

  • Online Form — Fill out our contact form with "Volunteer" in the subject line
  • Direct Email — Email nitu@miamcharitabletrust.com with your interest
  • Regional Coordinators — We'll connect you with the coordinator for your nearest area

We have opportunities for both on-ground work (in field) and remote support (online mentoring, social media, content creation).

What volunteer opportunities are available? +

On-Ground Opportunities:

  • Teaching and mentoring at MIAM Academies
  • Environmental work (tree plantation, conservation)
  • Animal welfare (rescue, care, rehabilitation)
  • Community health and nutrition programs
  • Women empowerment workshops

Remote Opportunities:

  • Online mentoring for exam preparation
  • Content creation and storytelling
  • Social media management
  • Data analysis and reporting
  • Fundraising and donor engagement
Do I need prior experience to volunteer? +

No. We welcome volunteers from all backgrounds. What matters most is your willingness to learn and contribute meaningfully.

Whether you're a student wanting summer experience, a professional taking a sabbatical, or a retiree giving back—there's a role for you at Mïam.

We provide training and mentorship to help you succeed in your volunteer role.

How long do I need to commit? +

Commitment periods vary based on your availability and the program:

  • Short-term — Weekend volunteers, one-day events (tree planting, fundraisers)
  • Medium-term — 1-3 month programs (seasonal initiatives, summer programs)
  • Long-term — 6+ months for deeper impact (mentoring, program coordination)

We're flexible and work with your schedule. Even 2-3 hours per week makes a real difference!

Will I receive a certificate? +

Yes. All volunteers who complete their commitment period receive a formal certificate of appreciation detailing their hours and contributions.

This is useful for:

  • Resumé building (especially for students and job seekers)
  • University admission portfolios
  • Professional development records
  • Documentation of social impact work

assessment Impact & Transparency

What impact has Mïam made so far? +

Since founding in 2021, Mïam has:

  • Helped tribal youth secure government jobs in police and defense services
  • Improved education access in 50+ remote villages across Maharashtra
  • Planted 10,000+ trees and distributed 5,000+ fruit tree saplings
  • Rescued and rehabilitated 1,000+ animals
  • Empowered 2,000+ women through skill development programs
  • Provided nutritional support and healthcare to 5,000+ community members

These numbers continue to grow as we expand our footprint.

How does Mïam ensure transparency? +

We're committed to radical transparency:

  • Detailed Financial Reports — Annual breakdowns of income, expenses, and fund allocation
  • Photographic Evidence — Regular photos of projects and activities
  • GPS-Tagged Projects — Geolocation verification of environmental work
  • Impact Updates — Monthly newsletters and progress reports to donors
  • Third-Party Audits — Independent verification of our operations
  • Government Registration — Compliance with 80G, 12A, and NITI Aayog requirements

You can trust that your donation is making real impact.

Can I visit a Mïam project in person? +

Absolutely. We welcome donors, CSR partners, and supporters to visit our projects on the ground.

You can visit:

  • MIAM Academy in Nashik or Gadchiroli
  • Active tree plantation sites
  • Animal rehabilitation centers
  • Community health camps and women empowerment programs

Contact us to arrange a site visit. We typically ask for 2-4 weeks notice and provide local accommodation guidance.
