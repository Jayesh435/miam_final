Who We Are

The Heart of Mïam

At Mïam Charitable Trust, we believe that true progress is achieved only when humanity, nature, and deep-rooted cultural values move forward together. Founded in 2021 by Nitu Joshi, our organization is a beacon of hope for the marginalized and voiceless across rural Maharashtra.

Our work is not merely about charity; it is about building sustainable, self-reliant communities. From the dense forests of Gadchiroli to the tribal belts of Nashik, we are on the ground every day, fighting for a more equitable world.

Our Core Principles

Three Pillars of Change

मानवता

Manvta (Humanity)

Humanity is the greatest, most defining quality of our species. It is the invisible thread that connects us to our fellow human beings, as well as the animals and birds that share our world. At Mïam, we believe that an act of kindness, no matter how small, sends ripples through society.

We empower the youth with these exact qualities, teaching them that true strength lies in lifting others up and showing mercy to the voiceless. Whether it is providing a hot meal to an elderly widow or rescuing a wounded animal, humanity is at the forefront of every action we take.

संस्कृती

Sanskruti (Cultural Heritage)

Culture plays an incredibly major role in connecting diverse people and transforming the human experience. It provides us with a sense of identity, belonging, and shared history. We promote lasting social harmony through community bonding, fostering a sense of unbreakable togetherness.

Through our 'उत्सव का संकल्प' (Joy of Resolution) initiatives, we organize vibrant festival celebrations in tribal areas. By keeping traditional arts, dances, and customs alive, we ensure that the youth remain deeply connected to their roots while navigating the modern world.

संस्कार

Sanskar (Values & Ethics)

Value Education (Sanskar) plays a vital, non-negotiable role in both the outer behavior and the inner psychological development of our youth. These foundational values form the bedrock of human bonding to build a secure, empathetic society.

At our MIAM Academies, we don't just teach syllabus content; we impart moral education. We train young minds to be respectful, disciplined, and fiercely dedicated to national development. A society built on strong 'Sanskar' is resilient to the challenges of tomorrow.

flag
Our Mission

Miam Charitable Trust is a dedicated, rapidly developing NGO making a measurable, positive impact on human lives, animal welfare, and environmental health every single year.

We focus our resources on Adivasi (indigenous) empowerment, rural development, and providing the tools necessary for self-reliance. By facilitating access to government schemes, healthcare, and education, we are bridging the gap between isolated communities and mainstream opportunities.

visibility
Our Vision

We operate on the fundamental truth that humans, animals, and the natural environment are deeply and inextricably interconnected. A thriving ecosystem is essential for human prosperity.

Our vision is a future where marginalized communities take the reins in actively shaping their own successful futures. We dream of a world where no animal goes hungry, no youth is denied an education due to poverty, and our forests stand tall and protected for generations to come.

The People Behind

Founder & Trustee

Nitu Joshi

Director — Ebina Group of Companies

emoji_events Best Social Worker Award — Mid-Day, 2023

Nitu Joshi is a seasoned "Business and Social Entrepreneur" with over 25 years of experience. She founded Aayush Telefilms and Vedansh Entertainment in 1998 to provide clean, family-oriented entertainment through TV and film, with content aired on major national channels. Her commitment to wholesome narratives laid the early groundwork for her transition into deep social work.

As Group Director of Ebina Group, she drives the success of Ebina Entertainment. Her recent powerful documentary, "Dharamraobaba Atram – Dilon Ka Raja," was released on Jio Cinema and won the prestigious Best Documentary award at the Cine Dreams International Film Festival 2024. The film highlights the life and struggles of a revered tribal leader, showcasing Nitu's dedication to bringing marginalized stories to the mainstream.

In 2021, fueled by a lifelong desire to create direct, tangible impact, she established the "Miam Charitable Trust". The trust was born to tackle pressing social, environmental, and socio-cultural issues head-on. Nitu lives by a simple but profound philosophy: "dosro ki liye bhi jeena sikho" (learning to live for others).

Under her relentless leadership, Mïam has expanded its footprint across multiple districts in Maharashtra, touching thousands of lives and proving that with enough compassion and strategic action, systemic change is possible.
